Likhon for Mac: how to install
==============================

Likhon is a free Bangla phonetic keyboard (ami -> আমি). It needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, on Apple Silicon
or Intel. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

1. Open "Install Likhon.pkg".
   This copy is not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS may say it cannot be opened. Then:
   Control-click (right-click) the file, choose Open, and choose Open again. Or: System Settings > Privacy &
   Security > scroll down > "Open Anyway".
2. Click Continue and Install, and type your Mac password when asked.
3. Log out and log back in (macOS finds new keyboards at login).
4. System Settings > Keyboard > Input Sources > Edit... > + > Bangla > Likhon > Add.
5. Switch with the Input menu in the menu bar, or Control+Space.

To remove it: delete "Likhon.app" from /Library/Input Methods, remove Likhon from Input Sources, and log out.

Help: https://likhon.agniflux.com/support
